Facilities What the club includes

Everything on the water

A club designed around the cove, with activities for the whole family and for those seeking serious nautical sport.

Pier and mooring buoys

Authorized water area for small vessels, with seven mooring points in service.

Sea-water pool

A protected natural pool, using the cove's conditions for safe swimming and water activities.

Restaurant and bar

Seafood cuisine on the water, with priority reservations for members.

Sport fishing

A platform equipped for sport fishing in the cove and its surroundings.

Serviced camping

An equipped camping area, with priority access for family-membership holders.

Own water and power

The club is part of the property with its own water license and SEAL electrical feasibility.
